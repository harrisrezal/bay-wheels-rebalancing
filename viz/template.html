What you're looking at. Ebikes are 82% of all Bay Wheels trips, so a station holding only classic bikes is serving under a fifth of demand while counting as stocked. Across this window 22.9% of station-observations had zero ebikes while open for business — against 7.4% with no bikes at all. The gap between those two numbers is invisible to any metric that counts bikes.
